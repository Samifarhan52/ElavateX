Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before kicking off a project with ElavateX.

How quickly will we hear back?

During studio hours (Mon – Sat, 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM IST), inquiries via WhatsApp are answered within 15 to 45 minutes. Form submissions are reviewed and answered within 2 hours maximum.

Can we sign an NDA before sharing detailed specs?

Absolutely. We uphold strict client confidentiality by default. If required, we can sign your company NDA or provide our standard mutual NDA prior to any deep architectural discussions.

How do retainers vs fixed-price projects work?

For defined MVP scopes, we work on transparent fixed-price milestones. For scaling products requiring continuous weekly features and optimization, we offer dedicated monthly senior developer retainers.

Can we see live examples of previous work?

Yes! Explore our live production deployments including Azalea Tourism Doha Qatar (azaleaconsultancy.com), HelloBotz, Trait Innovation, and The Edu Consultant in our portfolio.
