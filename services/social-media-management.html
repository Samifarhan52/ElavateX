FAQ

Got Questions?

Find quick answers about our processes, contracts, and delivery details.

How does the content calendar review work?

We design and schedule all posts in a custom digital calendar board at the start of the month. You review, suggest any copy tweaks, and approve them before anything is queued for publishing.

Do you manage responding to direct message requests?

For basic questions, we construct custom automated chat routing prompts (linking users to WhatsApp or your website CRM). For direct client inquiries, we route them instantly to your inbox.

Is there a setup fee or lock-in contract duration?

No hidden setup fees. All packages operate on rolling monthly subscriptions. You are free to upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your renewal plan at any time without penalty.

How long does it take to start posting?

Once we complete the kickoff consultation and brand onboarding, our strategy team delivers your first content batch calendar in 5–7 business days.
